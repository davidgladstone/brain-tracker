# Define multi-Memory Knowledge Variant identity and synthesis

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Labels: wayfinder:grilling

## Question

When several related Memories are relevant to an Audience, when should they become one Knowledge Variant versus several variants linked by Areas or Relations?

## Comments

### Resolution

One Knowledge Variant is appropriate when its contributing Artifacts form a coherent retrieval unit for the Audience. Distinct retrieval intents, incompatible topics, materially different time states, or disagreements that cannot be presented coherently should become separate Variants. The Curator may group sources across Memories and Realms after Artifact-level eligibility filtering.

Every contributing Artifact remains a direct dependency and determines part of the Variant’s effective classification. Exact duplicates may share one Variant while retaining every source reference. A changed, withdrawn, or newly unauthorized Artifact invalidates each dependent Variant for re-curation; the replacement may retain its Publication-scoped ID only when the subject remains the same. Splits and merges receive new IDs.

Decide the unit of synthesis, stable identity across source updates and re-curation, topic boundaries, and split/merge/retire behavior. Cover a document supported by sources from multiple Realms, a Memory that becomes irrelevant, and two sources that overlap or contradict one another. The result must keep Knowledge Variant content subordinate to canonical Artifacts and define the contributing-source set used for authorization and invalidation.
