# Define Area placement, hierarchy, and summary semantics

Type: grilling
Status: open
Labels: wayfinder:grilling
Blocked by: 02, 05

## Question

What is the durable contract for the hierarchical Areas that index Knowledge Variants for an Audience?

Decide how variants are placed in one or more Areas, how the root and child hierarchy are created or reorganized, what each Area summary is allowed to say, and how summaries are regenerated bottom-up without leaking denied descendants or thrashing on unchanged meaning. Cover singleton Areas, empty Areas, cross-cutting topics, human-preferred placement, and multi-audience profile roots. The result must distinguish navigation summaries from Knowledge Variant content and define the publication validation invariants.
