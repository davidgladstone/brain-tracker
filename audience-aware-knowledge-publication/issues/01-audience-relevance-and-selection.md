# Define audience relevance and source selection

Type: grilling
Status: open
Labels: wayfinder:grilling
Blocked by: 05, 06

## Question

What exactly makes an authorized Artifact relevant enough for an Audience's Knowledge Projection?

Decide how the Audience curation brief is interpreted, whether relevance is assessed per Artifact, Memory, or candidate topic, and how a Memory with both eligible and denied Artifacts is represented. Cover ordinary source updates, multiple related Memories, irrelevance, duplicate or near-duplicate material, and the boundary between deterministic authorization and model-assisted editorial selection. The result must define what source material can be shown to a curation model and what evidence is retained for the selection decision.
